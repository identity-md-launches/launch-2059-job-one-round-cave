# The Values in the Basket

A worn ochre basket holds three differently shaped charcoal stones. A short
trail leads into it: the container is useful only when its values are checked.
It accompanies MapCheck, a standard-library extension for OpenAPI
additionalProperties value comparisons.

Built-in imagegen was used to create the pigment. Final prompt:

> Use case: historical-scene. Create a tiny isolated prehistoric cave-pigment motif for compositing onto existing rough rock: a loosely woven red-ochre basket outline holding three small charcoal pebbles of different shapes, with a short yellow ochre trail leading into it. Primitive uneven burnt-stick and thumb marks, worn and grainy, sparse earth pigment only. Transparent background, no rock, no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no hands, no border. This represents checking values held in a map. Save the PNG within artifacts/line-4/wall-v18.png if filesystem saving is available.

The generated pigment was recolored to ochre and charcoal and blended onto
bare lower-left stone at `[170, 925, 250, 160]` (x, y, width, height). Only 4,666
pixels changed; every pixel outside that rectangle is identical to record 36's
wall. The inherited wall was verified by its record's SHA-256 before use.
No ancestor was erased, moved or redrawn. The raw motif was overwritten with
the full finished wall; no extra draft image remains in the workspace.

Final wall: `artifacts/line-4/wall-v18.png` and `artifacts/line-4/wall.png`,
**1254 × 1254, RGB PNG, 1,720,233 bytes**. SHA-256:
`962a7a2b80d000e96ebd7739df0576c08d8616ce6b23753376e5a77b0ee5a81a`.
The preview is **512 × 512 JPEG, 88,102 bytes** at
`dist/line-4/37.jpg`, hard-linked to `artifacts/line-4/37.jpg` to satisfy the
explicit record-preview location. Record: `dist/line-4/37.json`.

Visual inspection of both final files: Pepe remains a cave figure with a wide
mouth and heavy-lidded eyes. Both inherited hands have four fingers and one
thumb; the new mark adds no hands or stencils. No letters, numbers, captions,
signatures, logos, watermarks, frames, real people or political/hateful symbols
were observed. Earlier marks, cracks, warm lighting and framing remain exact
outside the addition. The inherited rock texture is photographic in detail;
it was preserved as required. Small stones and basket grain soften in the
512px preview. No other unmet visual requirement was observed.
